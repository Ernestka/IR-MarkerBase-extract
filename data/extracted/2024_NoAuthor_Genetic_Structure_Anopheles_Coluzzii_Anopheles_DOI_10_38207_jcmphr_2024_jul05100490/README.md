### Extraction Summary

- **Study Design**: The study evaluated the genetic structure and resistance marker frequencies (Vgsc L995F/`kdr-L995F` and Ace-1 G280S/`ace-1 G280S`) in *Anopheles gambiae* s.s. and *Anopheles coluzzii* collected monthly across five communes in northern Benin (Bembèrèkè [control], Kandi, Gogounou, Djougou, and Copargo) across three intervention years (2019: Pirimiphos-methyl 300 CS; 2020: Clothianidin 500 g/kg + Deltamethrin 62.5 g/kg; 2021: Clothianidin 50 WG).
- **Surveys**: Split into 30 distinct surveys by site (5 communes) x collection year (2019, 2020, 2021) x molecular species (*An. gambiae* s.s. and *An. coluzzii*).
