### Extraction Summary
- **Study Design**: Cluster-randomized trial evaluating mass screening and treatment of asymptomatic malaria carriers in Saponé health district, Burkina Faso, with a parallel entomological study conducted throughout 2011.
- **Surveys**: Mosquitoes were collected weekly via indoor pyrethrum spray catches in 10 clusters (5 intervention, 5 control). Data are split by trial arm (Intervention vs Control) as presented in Table 2.
- **Genotypes**: A total of 500 An. gambiae s.l. (50 per cluster x 10 clusters = 250 per arm) were genotyped for the kdr West African mutation (Vgsc L995F / L1014F, mapped as marker kdr-west) using PCR (Martinez-Torres et al. 1998 / Ranson et al. 2000). Only cluster-level mean allele frequencies are reported in Table 2 (0.65 for intervention, 0.71 for control); raw genotype counts (RR/RS/SS) were not printed.
- **Bioassays**: No insecticide bioassays were performed in this study.
