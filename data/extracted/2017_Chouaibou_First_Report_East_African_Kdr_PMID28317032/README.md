## Decisions

### Survey and Sampling
- Mosquitoes were collected as larvae in October 2015 from rice fields on the outskirts of Yamoussoukro, Côte d'Ivoire (6°49′13″ N, 5°16′36″ W).
- A total of 50 unexposed control mosquitoes were drawn for species identification via SINE-PCR, of which 41 were successfully identified (28 An. coluzzii, 8 An. gambiae s.s., and 3 M/S hybrids). Note: 28 + 8 + 3 = 39, which leaves 2 specimens unassigned among the 41 identified, though percentages (68.3%, 19.5%, 7.3%) are calculated relative to N=41.

### Bioassays
- Bioassays were performed on wild-reared 3–5 day old adult females using standard WHO tube assays with 100 exposed mosquitoes per insecticide.
- Only percentage mortalities were reported (deltamethrin 1.03%, bendiocarb 38.46%, DDT 0%, malathion 92.47%); per instructions, exact counts were left as null and dead_alive_printed set to false.
- Laboratory reference strain (Kisumu) controls were excluded.

### Genotyping
- A subsample of 30 unexposed mosquitoes was genotyped for the East-kdr mutation (1014S); 1 single hybrid (M/S) mosquito was heterozygous (RS) and 29 were homozygous susceptible (SS).
- The 1 RS mosquito was further genotyped for West-kdr (1014F) and was found to be homozygous susceptible (SS).
