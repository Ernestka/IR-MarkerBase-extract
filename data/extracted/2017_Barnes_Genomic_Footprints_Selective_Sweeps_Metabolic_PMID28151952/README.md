## Decisions

This study analyzes continent-wide population structure and selective sweeps around the rp1 pyrethroid resistance QTL locus in Anopheles funestus s.s. using microsatellites (AFUB6, FunR, etc.), Sanger sequencing of BAC fragments and the CYP6P9a gene, and whole-genome pool sequencing (POOLseq). 

No direct raw genotype counts (RR, RS, SS) or individual allele frequency calls for target markers (such as Cyp6p9a_R SNP) were reported for individual field surveys in the main text; instead, full 1.9kb CYP6P9a gene haplotypes and microsatellite allele diversity were analyzed. No bioassays were conducted in this study (bioassay references in text are citations of previous publications). All 11 mosquito collection surveys across Benin, Cameroon, Ghana, Malawi, Mozambique, and Uganda are extracted.
