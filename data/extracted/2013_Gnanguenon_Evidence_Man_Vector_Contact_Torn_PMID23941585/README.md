## Decisions

### Study and Sampling Design
- Mosquitoes were caught entering torn bed nets (with 12 standardized holes, proportionate Hole Index = 276) hung in selected houses in three bioclimatic zones of Benin: Abomey-Takplikpo (Adjarra district), Bame (Zangnanado district), and Malanville (Malanville district).
- Three net types were evaluated concurrently at each site: Non-insecticidal control nets, PE-150D LLINs (Olyset, permethrin incorporated), and PET-75D LLINs (PermaNet 2.0, deltamethrin coated).
- Mosquitoes were collected continuously inside the nets throughout the night (21:00 to 05:00) using aspirators. Captured mosquitoes were unfed and collected alive as they entered.
- Surveys are separated by site and net type (3 sites x 3 net types = 9 surveys). Total sample sizes correspond to the total An. gambiae s.l. caught in each net type at each site reported in Table 2 (total N = 1,076).

### Genotyping
- A subset of An. gambiae s.l. females was tested for the kdr mutation (L1014F / Vgsc L995F) using PCR-RFLP following Martinez-Torres et al. (1998) (ref [27]) (approx. 20% sampled from the resistance sites Abomey-Takplikpo and Bame, and approx. 40% from Malanville, giving a grand total of 320 genotyped females).
- The target marker name is specified as Vgsc L1014F (canonical target: Vgsc L995F).
- Complete genotype classes (SS, RS, RR), sample sizes (n_genotyped), and reported allele frequencies F(Kdr) are directly provided in Table 3 on page 8.
- No bioassays (WHO susceptibility or CDC bottle assays) or genotype-phenotype splits were conducted in this study; hence bioassays and geno_pheno arrays are empty.
