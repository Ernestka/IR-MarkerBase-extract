## Decisions

All mosquitoes collected were molecularly identified as An. gambiae s.s. M form (now An. coluzzii). Table 1 provides genotype counts for kdr L1014F and ace-1 G119S in adults collected via HLC across 9 zones (Control IRS, Control LLIN, Adjohoun IRS, Dangbo IRS, Misserete IRS, Seme IRS, Adjohoun LLIN, Dangbo LLIN, Seme LLIN) across 3 years (2007, 2009, 2010). Tables 2 and 3 provide kdr L1014F genotypes in deltamethrin bioassay survivors and dead specimens reared from larval collections across 6 localities and 3 years. Bioassay mortality data are presented in Figure 2 only as bar graphs with significance brackets without printed counts or percentages, so no separate bioassays are extracted and bioassay_id in geno_pheno is set to null with insecticide 'deltamethrin'. Table 3 contains two rows where printed genotype counts do not sum to the reported N: Misserete IRS 2009 dead (22+6+3=31 vs N=30; printed freq 0.81 matches N=31) and Misserete IRS 2010 dead (18+4+3=25 vs N=22; printed freq 0.80 matches N=25). Both discrepancies are documented in their respective inconsistency_note fields.
