### Genotyping Decisions
- **Marker**: The paper refers to the West African mutation as 'leucine to phenylalanine L1014P' on page 3 (a typographical error for L1014F) and as 'kdr-w' on page 7. We record gene as 'kdr' and marker as 'kdr-w'.
- **Counts**: The paper reports that for 2 samples there was no amplification of the kdr gene, and all the rest (95.2%) were homozygous resistant (RR), with the exception of 1 (4.8%) heterozygous resistant (RS). The percentages 95.2% and 4.8% correspond mathematically to 20/21 and 1/21. Out of 23 captured mosquitoes, the 2 non-amplified samples correspond to the 2 non-gambiae specimens (or 21 gambiae were successfully genotyped). The single RS mosquito is specifically noted on page 7 and Figure 4 to be the single M form (An. coluzzii) collected from the Plot su cluster, meaning all 20 S form mosquitoes were RR.
