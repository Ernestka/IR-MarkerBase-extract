### Study Overview
Badolo et al. (2012) developed an allele-specific loop-mediated isothermal amplification (AS-LAMP) method to detect the L1014F kdr-w mutation in Anopheles gambiae s.l. and evaluated its performance against AS-PCR and direct sequencing using 120 field-collected mosquitoes from Burkina Faso.

### Spatial and Temporal Resolution

- Collection dates/years are not reported in the text; temporal fields are left null with a note indicating publication in July 2012.

### Species Breakdown
- The 120 mosquitoes were identified using PCR-RFLP as 47% Anopheles arabiensis and 53% Anopheles gambiae s.s. (of which 15% were S form and 85% M form). However, the kdr genotype results are reported only for the pooled An. gambiae s.l. sample of 120 individuals, not disaggregated by species or molecular form. Therefore, a single survey record for Anopheles gambiae s.l. was created.
